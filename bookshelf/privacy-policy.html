1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Bookshelf ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by RizTech. Bookshelf is a creative digital notebook and journal management application designed for Android devices (Package ID: com.riztech.bookshelf).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data you create, view, or store inside Bookshelf. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the application accesses, how it is handled, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA).

Core Guarantee: Bookshelf is designed with an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, view, rent, or sell your personal notes, sketches, journal entries, or private sketches.

Bookshelf requests device permissions only when strictly necessary to provide features you actively choose to use. Below is an itemized breakdown of data and permissions:

3. Google Drive Integration & Cloud Sync

Bookshelf provides an optional cloud backup and restore feature powered by Google Drive. When you choose to use this feature:

  • Direct Communication: All data transfers occur directly between the Bookshelf application on your device and Google's official Drive APIs (via HTTPS/TLS).
  • Limited Scope (drive.file): Bookshelf only accesses files that were specifically created by the App or uploaded to your App's dedicated backup folder. Bookshelf cannot read, view, modify, or delete your unrelated Google Drive documents or personal files.
  • No Credential Storing: Authentication is handled by official Google Identity Services / OAuth 2.0 dialogs. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Third-Party Policy: Any data transferred to Google Drive is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services & Negative Disclosures

To ensure total transparency and comply with Google Play User Data policies, we provide the following explicit declarations:

  • No Third-Party Advertising: We do NOT include advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, ironSource). Your app experience is 100% ad-free.
  • No Behavioral Trackers: We do NOT embed third-party analytics trackers or surveillance SDKs (e.g., Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, Adjust).
  • No Sale or Leasing of Data: We do NOT sell, lease, rent, trade, or distribute your personal data or user content to any third parties, brokers, or marketing firms under any circumstance.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement robust technical measures to safeguard your information:

  • Local Sandboxing: All journal data, drawings, and settings are kept in the private application sandbox provided by the Android OS, preventing unauthorized access by other installed applications.
  • Encrypted Transmission: When syncing to Google Drive, all network communications utilize strong transport layer encryption (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
  • Data Retention: Because your data resides locally on your device, it remains there until you delete specific books, clear application storage, or uninstall the app.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion Policy

Under Google Play policies, GDPR, and CCPA, you retain complete sovereignty over your data at all times. You have the following capabilities:

  • Delete Individual Books & Pages: Use the built-in "Delete Books" mode in the Bookshelf Settings menu or the page trash button to permanently erase content from your device.
  • Disconnect & Delete Cloud Backups: You can disconnect your Google Account at any time via the Google Drive modal inside Bookshelf. You can also view and delete your Bookshelf backup files directly in your Google Drive storage.
  • Complete Local Erasure: To wipe all local books, templates, and settings immediately, navigate to Android Settings → Apps → Bookshelf → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Data Inquiries & Deletion Assistance: If you have any questions or require assistance deleting any associated metadata, you may contact the developer directly at sapuzer@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Bookshelf is a general-audience creative productivity application suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because Bookshelf does not collect personal identifiers or operate remote account servers, children's personal data is not processed on developer infrastructure.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information without parental consent, please contact us at sapuzer@gmail.com, and we will promptly assist with data deletion.
